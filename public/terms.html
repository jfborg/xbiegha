Your final review and printing

Inspect the complete original and final file at full size. Confirm the correct document, submission route, dimensions, background, framing, resolution, recency, file format and file limits. Retain the unaltered original. Some authorities require live capture, an approved booth, a professional photographer, particular paper, endorsements or original prints; an app export cannot fulfil those conditions by itself.

Printer settings, paper, ink, colour handling and application uploads can alter an image. Print PDF sheets at 100% / Actual size without fit-to-page, measure the physical result, and follow all paper and endorsement rules. Do not rely solely on an app result when paying application fees, arranging travel or making other time-sensitive or non-refundable commitments. The receiving authority alone decides acceptance.
