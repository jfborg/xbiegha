A LITTLE CLARITY

Privacy & local storage

Effective 30 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-30

Your selected photographs are processed in your browser. Xbiegha’s photo tools do not send those images to an application server or external AI service.

Photos, face analysis and projects

The app reads a photograph only when you select, take, drop or import it. Editing, rendering, face landmarks, eyewear and other document-photo checks run locally. The models and processing code are downloaded from this site. Photos and face-analysis results are not sent to a model provider, used to identify you against a database, or collected by Xbiegha to train models.

Project data, including photo content, crop settings, manually positioned guides, the capture date you enter and a thumbnail, is saved automatically in this browser’s IndexedDB. Automatic detector scores and the export acknowledgement are held in the current page’s memory rather than sent to a server. Closing or reloading the page clears that temporary analysis. Opening a saved project runs a fresh check.

You can use the collage and editor without document-photo analysis. Document-photo exports require the local checker and your review. Do not import an entire passport, identity card or other sensitive document: the tool needs only the portrait.

Control and retention on your device

Saved projects remain until you delete them in My projects, clear this site’s browser data, or your browser removes them. Browser storage is not an encrypted vault or a backup and may be accessible to someone using the same device and browser profile. Use a trusted device. Downloaded images and editable backups remain wherever you save them, may include original photo data or metadata, and must be removed separately. Xbiegha cannot retrieve, restore or remotely delete local files.

Saving and sharing your finished image

If you choose Save to Photos, the app hands the finished image to your device’s sharing interface. You choose whether to save it locally or share it with another app or service. Any destination you select, including a synced photo library, handles that image under its own settings and privacy practices. Xbiegha cannot see or confirm the final destination. The normal image download remains available.

Hosting and technical requests

Vercel hosts the website. Loading the site and its assets necessarily sends ordinary technical request information to the hosting infrastructure, which can include an IP address, requested URL, time, browser/device headers, referring site and security or diagnostic information. These requests do not include the portrait selected in the editor. We do not include photos, entered capture dates or local project contents in request URLs or application telemetry.

Technical data is processed to deliver and secure the site, prevent abuse and diagnose faults, based on legitimate interests in operating a reliable and secure service, and legal obligations where applicable. Hosting records are retained under the provider’s applicable service, security and legal retention arrangements; this site does not maintain a separate visitor-profile database. Vercel operates internationally, including in the United States. Its privacy notice and data-processing terms describe its processing and international-transfer safeguards.

Cookies, tracking and external links

The photo application does not set advertising or analytics cookies, include third-party analytics scripts, or sell or share portraits for advertising. Essential local project storage and normal browser caching support the functions you use. Fonts, demonstration images and checker models are served from this site. Browser extensions, device backup/sync services and websites you visit through external links operate independently and have their own privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

Where applicable, data-protection law provides rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, subject to its conditions. For project content, you directly control access, editing, download and deletion in your browser; we do not hold a server copy to search or supply. You may complain to your local data-protection authority, including Malta’s Information and Data Protection Commissioner where applicable. This notice does not waive your rights.

Age and changes

The studio is intended for adults aged 18 or over. Standard adult document-photo measurements must not be used for children or babies. This notice describes the current app version. Any material change to uploads, tracking or photo processing will require an updated notice before that processing begins.
