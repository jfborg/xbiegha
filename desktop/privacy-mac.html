YOUR MAC EDITION

Your photos. At home.

Mac edition · 4 November 2026

Xbieha processes your photographs, movies and audio on this Mac. The installed app does not upload them to an application server or external AI service.

Your library is a folder on disk

Projects are editable .xbieha files in ~/Library/Application Support/Xbieha/Library/Projects. Video drafts and copied source files live in the Library folder’s Video and Media subfolders. Open it using Xbieha → Show project folder. Project content is not saved in browser storage. Clearing Safari or Chrome data does not remove this library.

Saving keeps the previous saved version where available. Removing a photo project moves its files into the library’s Trash folder. The video editor retains sources needed by its current and previous draft; older unused copies may be removed. Your original files outside the library are never changed. Copy the whole Library folder to a separate backup drive while Xbieha is closed to protect both projects and movie sources. A single .xbieha file is an editable backup for a photo or slideshow project.

Local photo analysis

The document-photo checker and its models are bundled with this app. Face landmarks, eyewear and other checks run on your Mac. Results are not sent to a model provider, used to identify you against a database, or collected by Xbieha to train models. Temporary detector scores and export acknowledgements are kept in memory; opening a saved project runs a fresh assessment. Do not import an entire passport, identity card or other sensitive document to obtain a portrait photo.

Exports and external links

Native save windows let you choose where finished images, PDF sheets, videos and editable backups go. If you choose a folder managed by iCloud Drive, OneDrive or another sync service, that service may upload the exported file independently. Photos and other apps you use to open exports have their own storage settings.

The installed studio uses bundled fonts, samples and checker models. It has no analytics, advertising cookies, automatic updater or background web requests. Official reference links open your normal web browser only when you click them; those sites have their own privacy practices. Your operating system may maintain normal application caches, crash reports and backups. Anyone with access to your Mac account or its backups may be able to read your project files.

Your choices

You control your local files, backups and exports. Xbieha holds no server copy to recover or supply. Use your Mac’s account protection, disk encryption and backup settings as appropriate for your photographs. This notice does not waive any applicable privacy rights. The studio’s existing terms and document-photo limitations continue to apply.
